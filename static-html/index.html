FAQ

Honest answers for skeptical brokers.

Is this really AI, or a human in a call centre?

It's AI, and we always say so. If a client asks whether they're speaking to a person, the agent answers honestly and offers a human.

What happens if the AI can't answer?

It never guesses. It tells the client a specialist will follow up, and instantly notifies the right agent with the full conversation.

Is my data secure?

Data is encrypted in transit and at rest, hosted in the EU, and fully GDPR compliant. We never train shared models on your clients' data.

Will it work with my CRM?

We integrate natively with the major real estate CRMs, and offer an API and Zapier for everything else.

How long does setup take?

Most agencies go live within 5 working days, with a dedicated onboarding specialist.
